Exams ask for facts: who, what, where, when and how many.
Category	Example of what to write
Appointments	Who became the new head of an organisation or post
Awards and honours	Award name, winner, field
Sports	Tournament, winner, venue, record
Government schemes	Scheme name, ministry, aim, launch date
Economy and banking	Repo rate, GDP growth figure, budget highlights, new banking rules
Summits and international	Summit name, host city or country, theme
Reports and rankings	Index name, publisher, India’s rank
Science, tech and defence	ISRO missions, defence exercises with partner countries
Important days	Date and that year’s theme
Books and authors	Title and author
